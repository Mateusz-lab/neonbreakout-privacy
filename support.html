Neon Breakout Support

Neon Breakout is an offline brick breaker for Android and iPhone by Codedzilla.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas: codedzilla834@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

When reporting a bug, please include your device model, system version and what you did just before the problem.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere to move the paddle.
  • Tap to launch the ball.
  • Catch falling letters for power-ups: W (wide paddle), M (multi-ball), S (slow motion), + (extra life).
  • Tap the pause button in the top right corner to pause. The game also pauses when you leave the app.

Common questions

Does the game need the internet? No. It works fully offline.

Where is my high score kept? Only on your device. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy policy.
