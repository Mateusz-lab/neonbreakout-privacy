Privacy Policy for Neon Breakout

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

1. Overview

Thank you for playing Neon Breakout ("the Game"), developed by Codedzilla. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you play our mobile application available on the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.

2. Information Collection and Use

Neon Breakout does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information.

Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not collect location data (neither precise nor coarse GPS data).
  • We do not collect device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA). The Game does not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not utilize any third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase).
  • We do not display targeted advertisements or share data with ad brokers.

3. Local Data Storage

The Game only saves your local game progress (high score and level records) directly on your device using the sandboxed local application storage of Android or iOS (user://best.save). This information never leaves your device and is automatically deleted if you uninstall the Game.

4. Permissions

Neon Breakout requires minimal device permissions to run:

  • VIBRATION (Haptics): Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g., when the ball bounces or a brick is broken).

On iPhone, haptic feedback needs no permission. The Game does not use the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or location.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Game does not address anyone under the age of 13 intentionally to collect personal information, nor do we knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
